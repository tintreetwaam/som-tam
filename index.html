Coffee

We source our coffee beans from Christchurch-based roasters Underground Coffee. We are the only provider of their premium, organic, Fair Trade Beatnik blend in Hanmer Springs.
Our experienced barista, Gun, honed his coffee making skills from working in cafes in Perth, Queentown and Wanaka for over a decade.

thai

Our kitchen team is 100% Thai with over a century of combined experience living and cooking in Thailand. Our menu includes dishes from diverse regions of the country and takes inspiration from the Thai street food stalls in Bangkok, Chiang Mai, Buriram and beyond. decade.
We have a small menu of Thai dishes, cooked fresh to order in the wok, providing a balance of the spicy, sweet, salty flavours that make Thai food unique. Our lunch menu contains a number of regional Thai dishes, including Khao Soi and Som Tam, that are not commonly found outside of Thailand.

About

Som Tam was started in 2024 by two Kiwis, Gun and Andrew, through their joint passion for coffee and Thai food. Gun hails from Buriram in the Isaan region of Thailand. He has been working as a barista and in Thai restaurants in Queenstown, Wanaka and Perth for over a decade.
Andrew has spent the past 20-years working as a scientist, conducting research in mathematics, space radar and oceanography in the UK, New Zealand, Thailand and Australia. We are joined at Som Tam by our Thai chefs, Num & Ar.
Our name, Som Tam, is the most famous dish from Gun’s home in the Isaan region of Thailand. It’s the ultimate Thai comfort food - the Thai equivalent of a Kiwi pie or fish & chips, but a tad more spicy. If you head into our kitchen after closing time then it’s likely the dish that our team will be eating together, laced with chillis & fermented fish sauce.
